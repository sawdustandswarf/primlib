Hinge (deprecated)

primlib/primitives/hinge_deprecated.py · class HingeDeprecated · all primitives

Deprecated: used only by latch. Don't use it for new work: the library's hinge is hinge (VerticalHinge3). This is the old ring hinge, kept until the latch moves to hinge.

A ring hinge along the z axis, printable in place. Five main specifications set its size and movement:

- Length (length): along the axis. - Leaf Length (leaf_length): the side plate. It starts on the barrel's tangent, barrel_diam / 2 from the axis, and runs at right angles to the axis, so a leaf ends barrel_diam / 2 + leaf_length from the axis. - Barrel Diameter (barrel_diam): the rings' outside diameter. - Pin Offsets (pin_offset_a, pin_offset_b): the axis to each leaf's mounting face. Equal offsets put both faces in one plane when the hinge is open flat; B follows A until edited. - Angle Range (angle_min, angle_max): the opening between the leaves, 0 folded back to back and 180 open flat. These are checked against what the leaves allow (angle_limit) but nothing printed stops the hinge yet, so it can still be turned past them. Where the other side's ring tapers to its tip, at each joint and hinge end, its clearance cut narrows to finger_r, leaving a thin finger of tab along the mounting face, and those fingers touch first. With the face outside finger_r they run almost to tab_u, so the limit is just past flat: 182.6 deg at the defaults. Moving the face inside finger_r opens it up quickly: about 224 deg at offset 5.5 and 245 at offset 5, with a 13 mm barrel.

A column of rings retained pins, one per ring, each rides in a sleeve. The rings alternate between side A (the bottom one) and side B. The pin column is captive -- its flares cannot pass the sleeve throats -- and spins freely in both sides.

Neighbouring rings stand end_gap apart at the joint. With ring_ends = 1 (pointed, the default) each ring is the sleeve shortened by end_gap / 2 at both ends, so its ends stay sharp ridges and two rings meet tip to tip end_gap apart; the hinge's own ends are end_gap / 2 in from z = 0 and length. With ring_ends = 0 the rings run the full length and a slab end_gap thick is cut out at each joint, leaving each ring end a flat land end_gap wide. Either way the bore is the pin's envelope, so the pin keeps its tolerance everywhere.

Each side has an interface: a rectangular tab, the hinge's length, joined to that side's rings and cut clear of the other side's rings by the sleeve clearance primitive -- the ring grown by roll_clearance normal to every face -- so the hinge turns. A tab runs from roll_clearance / 2 off the axis along the leaf direction pose_a / pose_b. Across the leaf, its mounting face lies pin_offset from the axis and the tab is iface_thick (iface_thick_b on side B) thick, behind that face; a tab may run past the axis, as it is cut clear of the pins and of the other side's rings. Side B is mirrored. pose_b = 180 deg builds it open flat, and an opening of X is pose_b = 360 deg - X.

With plates = 1 each tab ends on the tangent and carries a plate: the plate primitive, continuing the tab outward, flush with the mounting face, the hinge's length wide, screw heads away from the mounting face. With plates = 0 the plate shrinks into its tab and the hinge is tabs only, each ending tab_reach beyond the ring's outside; it is a spreadsheet cell, so change it and recompute.

The result is a compound of three solids: side A, side B and the pins.

Use

import primlib
# appended to the document's Params sheet under "HingeDeprecated: Hinge (deprecated)",
# aliases HingeDeprecated_length, HingeDeprecated_leaf_length, ...
obj = primlib.make("hinge_deprecated", doc, "HingeDeprecated")
obj = primlib.make("hinge_deprecated", doc, "HingeDeprecated", length='40', leaf_length='25')   # starting values

# into another sheet, or with plain shared aliases (length, ...)
obj = primlib.make("hinge_deprecated", doc, "HingeDeprecated", sheet=sheet)
obj = primlib.make("hinge_deprecated", doc, "HingeDeprecated", prefix="")

# or drive params from expressions you already have
obj = primlib.make("hinge_deprecated", doc, "HingeDeprecated", sheet=sheet,
                 bind={"length": "MyAlias * 2"})

In the FreeCAD GUI: the primlib workbench has a button for Hinge (deprecated). Its parameters are appended to the document's Params sheet under a title row, or to the selected spreadsheet if one is selected.

Objects created: the spreadsheet rows, the hidden helper objects described above, and the result <name>, tagged PrimlibPrimitive = "hinge_deprecated". They are plain FreeCAD objects: the saved file does not need primlib.
