Rail end

primlib/primitives/rail_end.py · class RailEnd · all primitives

The end of the hybrid case's Topeak rail: the bottom Y edge over the rail's width (a box_edge, notched by notch underneath to clear the rack, no screws through its lower wall) fused to a topeak_rail tongue that runs from the case's outside face in under the bottom panel, so the rail's end is one piece with the edge above it.

The drop-on entry: the edges' 45 degree bevel is carried down through the support and tongue; from its foot on the tip, over drop_on, the tongue's flanks are cut back to a straight key (its tip edges chamfered by drop_chamfer, drop_clear inside the neck each side) that drops into the rack; over taper the cut narrows to nothing.

The tongue is end_length long: the corner's size, or what the entry needs if longer. Its two screw rows run up into the bottom panel, the first and last holes end_inset from its ends, past the bevel's foot by a head and 1 mm; they are cut again after the fuse, as the edge's wall lies over them.

Frame: x from the left panel's inside face (the outside face at -(panel_t + skin)), y = 0 on the rail's centre line, z = 0 the inside floor: the case's left end. The right end is the same turned half round about z. As the original hybrid case model builds it.

Use

import primlib
# appended to the document's Params sheet under "RailEnd: Rail end",
# aliases RailEnd_size, RailEnd_panel_t, ...
obj = primlib.make("rail_end", doc, "RailEnd")
obj = primlib.make("rail_end", doc, "RailEnd", size='27', panel_t='6.5')   # starting values

# into another sheet, or with plain shared aliases (size, ...)
obj = primlib.make("rail_end", doc, "RailEnd", sheet=sheet)
obj = primlib.make("rail_end", doc, "RailEnd", prefix="")

# or drive params from expressions you already have
obj = primlib.make("rail_end", doc, "RailEnd", sheet=sheet,
                 bind={"size": "MyAlias * 2"})

In the FreeCAD GUI: the primlib workbench has a button for Rail end. Its parameters are appended to the document's Params sheet under a title row, or to the selected spreadsheet if one is selected.

Objects created: the spreadsheet rows, the hidden helper objects described above, and the result <name>, tagged PrimlibPrimitive = "rail_end". They are plain FreeCAD objects: the saved file does not need primlib.
