Box corner

primlib/primitives/box_corner.py · class BoxCorner · all primitives

Three walls meeting at a corner, each the box_edge section carried on: a skin over each of the three panels, a web panel_gap wide where two panels' edges meet, and a bevel across each of the three outside edges. size here is the corner's own outside width, usually more than the edges'. Each panel has drill_rows by drill_rows countersunk holes.

Frame: the inside corner at the origin, the walls towards -x, -y and -z, each panel's inner face on x = 0, y = 0 or z = 0. The eight corners of a box are this part, mirrored.

It's built as three edge sections swept along x, y and z, so the small chamfer is part of the shape at every size: no feature picked from its edges can lose it. Their far ends are chamfered all round, then the three bevels are cut across the whole, with a flat triangular facet where they meet.

Glue line: with glue above 0, each panel's three faces here are cut back by glue, leaving ridges glue_ridge wide, as on box_edge. The panels sit on the ridges, exactly where they sit with no glue. Each pocket is open at the corner's far end, so glue and air can get out. Its sides are at 45 degrees.

Use

import primlib
# appended to the document's Params sheet under "BoxCorner: Box corner",
# aliases BoxCorner_panel_t, BoxCorner_skin, ...
obj = primlib.make("box_corner", doc, "BoxCorner")
obj = primlib.make("box_corner", doc, "BoxCorner", panel_t='6.5', skin='5')   # starting values

# into another sheet, or with plain shared aliases (panel_t, ...)
obj = primlib.make("box_corner", doc, "BoxCorner", sheet=sheet)
obj = primlib.make("box_corner", doc, "BoxCorner", prefix="")

# or drive params from expressions you already have
obj = primlib.make("box_corner", doc, "BoxCorner", sheet=sheet,
                 bind={"panel_t": "MyAlias * 2"})

In the FreeCAD GUI: the primlib workbench has a button for Box corner. Its parameters are appended to the document's Params sheet under a title row, or to the selected spreadsheet if one is selected.

Objects created: the spreadsheet rows, the hidden helper objects described above, and the result <name>, tagged PrimlibPrimitive = "box_corner". They are plain FreeCAD objects: the saved file does not need primlib.
