Rectangle support

primlib/primitives/support_rect.py · class SupportRect · all primitives

A print support under your own control, filling a rectangle length (x) by width (y), its corner at the origin, from the face below at z = 0 to the face it holds up at z = height.

Concentric rectangular walls, each wall_lines lines wide with gap_lines lines between them, stand between two interface layers, one layer_h layer each. As many rings as fit the short side are laid in from the outside, the innermost leaving at least a gap across the middle; when the middle is wider than a gap and a line, a middle line runs along the long side, so no span is much wider than a gap.

Each interface layer is single-line strips across the short side, interface_density of the area (0.33: a line in every three), laid in from both ends, with a middle strip when the middle gap is wider than the strip spacing, and a line round the outside joining the strips' ends. The top layer stops interface_gap under the supported face, and the bottom layer starts bottom_gap above z = 0 (0 on the bed), so the support breaks away after printing.

Built from the rings' sides, mitred at the corners: a Draft array of stripes in from each end, kept where that end is the nearest side of the rectangle (two triangles per direction), plus the middle line, which lies on the innermost ring when there is none; the interface strips are Draft arrays in from each end the same way.

Use

import primlib
# appended to the document's Params sheet under "SupportRect: Rectangle support",
# aliases SupportRect_length, SupportRect_width, ...
obj = primlib.make("support_rect", doc, "SupportRect")
obj = primlib.make("support_rect", doc, "SupportRect", length='20', width='10')   # starting values

# into another sheet, or with plain shared aliases (length, ...)
obj = primlib.make("support_rect", doc, "SupportRect", sheet=sheet)
obj = primlib.make("support_rect", doc, "SupportRect", prefix="")

# or drive params from expressions you already have
obj = primlib.make("support_rect", doc, "SupportRect", sheet=sheet,
                 bind={"length": "MyAlias * 2"})

In the FreeCAD GUI: the primlib workbench has a button for Rectangle support. Its parameters are appended to the document's Params sheet under a title row, or to the selected spreadsheet if one is selected.

Objects created: the spreadsheet rows, the hidden helper objects described above, and the result <name>, tagged PrimlibPrimitive = "support_rect". They are plain FreeCAD objects: the saved file does not need primlib.
