Support

primlib/primitives/support.py · class Support · all primitives

A print support under your own control: a wall length (x) by width (y) by thickness (z), its corner at the origin, pierced by a row of diamond holes. Put it between the bed (or a lower part) at z = 0 and the part it holds up at z = thickness; after printing, cut through the necks to separate them.

Sizes are in print units, from line_w and layer_h. Between the holes the wall is a row of hourglasses, narrowing at mid-height to a neck neck_lines lines wide -- the cut points. At 0 the hourglasses meet in lines, and the result is two solids, above and below the necks, touching only there. The holes are at least gap_lines lines wide at the necks: as many hourglasses as fit share length evenly. A skin of skin_layers layers along the bottom and top ties them into one wall and gives the part above a full face to print on.

The flanks are at 45 deg and meet at the neck, so a hole is a square diamond, as tall as it is wide; the wall is at least a hole and both skins tall, and the top skin takes whatever height is left, so the wall is exactly thickness tall.

Built as a box minus a fused Draft array of count + 1 diamond holes, each a sketch in the xz plane extruded along y; the first and last are centred on the ends, so the wall ends in half holes.

Use

import primlib
# appended to the document's Params sheet under "Support: Support",
# aliases Support_length, Support_width, ...
obj = primlib.make("support", doc, "Support")
obj = primlib.make("support", doc, "Support", length='30', width='1.2')   # starting values

# into another sheet, or with plain shared aliases (length, ...)
obj = primlib.make("support", doc, "Support", sheet=sheet)
obj = primlib.make("support", doc, "Support", prefix="")

# or drive params from expressions you already have
obj = primlib.make("support", doc, "Support", sheet=sheet,
                 bind={"length": "MyAlias * 2"})

In the FreeCAD GUI: the primlib workbench has a button for Support. Its parameters are appended to the document's Params sheet under a title row, or to the selected spreadsheet if one is selected.

Objects created: the spreadsheet rows, the hidden helper objects described above, and the result <name>, tagged PrimlibPrimitive = "support". They are plain FreeCAD objects: the saved file does not need primlib.
