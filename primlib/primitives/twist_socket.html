Twist socket

primlib/primitives/twist_socket.py · class TwistSocket · all primitives

The female half of a twist lock: a round boss body_d across and body_t deep under its face (z = 0), centred on the z axis, with a revolved taper socket in it. The socket's mouth is mouth_d across, its flank undercut at taper_angle to the face down to the floor depth below it, the floor corner rounded to radius and the mouth to radius - tol: a rail's dovetail, revolved, without the guide. lugs openings, each the lugs' lug_angle plus open_clr each side, cut the lip back to the floor radius so a twist_plug's lugs drop in, twist anticlockwise of where they lock (the plug turns clockwise to lock, seen from above). A detent bump per lug stands detent_h proud of the face, for the plug flange's dimples.

mouth_d is the mouth's sharp diameter (the lip's sharp inner edge, no rounds), not what calipers read, unlike rail15 and topeak_rail, so check a print against it with care.

The plug's lugs are lowered lead at their leading ends, so they start under the lips clear, and are preload too high along the rest: turning them home pulls the plug's flange down onto the face and centres it on the taper.

Use

import primlib
# appended to the document's Params sheet under "TwistSocket: Twist socket",
# aliases TwistSocket_mouth_d, TwistSocket_depth, ...
obj = primlib.make("twist_socket", doc, "TwistSocket")
obj = primlib.make("twist_socket", doc, "TwistSocket", mouth_d='30', depth='3.5')   # starting values

# into another sheet, or with plain shared aliases (mouth_d, ...)
obj = primlib.make("twist_socket", doc, "TwistSocket", sheet=sheet)
obj = primlib.make("twist_socket", doc, "TwistSocket", prefix="")

# or drive params from expressions you already have
obj = primlib.make("twist_socket", doc, "TwistSocket", sheet=sheet,
                 bind={"mouth_d": "MyAlias * 2"})

In the FreeCAD GUI: the primlib workbench has a button for Twist socket. Its parameters are appended to the document's Params sheet under a title row, or to the selected spreadsheet if one is selected.

Objects created: the spreadsheet rows, the hidden helper objects described above, and the result <name>, tagged PrimlibPrimitive = "twist_socket". They are plain FreeCAD objects: the saved file does not need primlib.
