Dovetail path

primlib/paths/dovetail.py · class Dovetail · all primitives and paths

The dovetail section of the rails and their housings: a tongue depth deep whose flanks lean out at angle to its tip face, so it is wider at the tip than at the root and can't pull out of its slot. It is width across where calipers read it (between vertical tangents to the tip radii), and everything else is worked from that. The tip corners are rounded tip_radius, the root corners root_radius.

Frame (docs/GLOSSARY.md): u across, centred on u = 0, sizes along it widths; v up, sizes along it heights. The root line is v = 0, the base above it and the tongue below, its tip at v = -depth (a rail hangs down from its support; depth is how far the tongue goes into its slot). A primitive maps (u, v) to its own plane: a rail's (y, z), or (r, z) about an axis for a revolved dovetail.

The outline is closed by a base: a rectangle base_width across and base_height high, standing on the root line (a rail's support, or the part of an object the tongue joins). It runs from the left root corner down the left flank, across the tip face, up the right flank to the right root corner, then round the base: anticlockwise, the tongue on its left. The root corners are rounded root_radius where the flanks turn onto the base's underside, so the base must be wider than the root and its rounds.

One offset gives every mate. points(grow, face) moves the flanks and the tip face grow normal to themselves (outwards when positive), and the base's faces face (its underside, the root line, down; its sides out; its top up), keeping the rounds concentric: the tip radius becomes tip_radius + grow, the root radius root_radius - face. A round of 0 or less is a sharp corner. So:

- the tongue itself: grow 0, face 0; - its slot, the tongue grown by a clearance c normal to every face, base and all: grow = face = c (the mating face moves down by c); - a lead-in, the dovetail shrunk by e while its base stays: grow = -e, face 0; a slot's lead-in, c + e and c.

widen is added to every u but the groove's, half to each side: a printer's width offset.

Option groove: a centre groove in the tip face, groove_width at the tip face, groove_depth deep, its sides at groove_angle. It takes the same offset (a slot's guide is the groove shrunk by c).

Status: Designed: no primitive draws it yet. tests/test_path.py holds it, base included, to the outlines rail15, topeak_rail and their housings draw today, number for number, with lead-ins, offsets and clearances.

Use

from primlib.core import Builder, Primitive
from primlib.paths.dovetail import Dovetail

D = Dovetail.declare()           # or declare(groove=True)

class Rail(Primitive):
    params = D.params + (...)        # or fixed = ..., to hold them
    derived = D.derived + (...)
    checks = D.checks + (...)

    @classmethod
    def build(cls, doc, name, sheet, env):
        b = Builder(doc, name, sheet, env)
        pts = Dovetail.points()                     # the tongue and its base
        # its slot: Dovetail.points(grow="clearance")
        # lead-in sections: Dovetail.points(grow="-end_taper", face="0")
        return b.finish(b.prism(None, pts, "yz", ("0", "length")))

A path makes no object of its own: the primitive's prism, loft or revolve sketch holds its points, bound to the sheet as any other.
