Twist plugtwist_plugThe male half of a twist lock, built locked in a `twist_socket` with the same parameters at the same origin: a flange `body_d` across and `flange_t` thick on the socket's face (z = 0 to `flange_t`), a shaft `tol` inside the mouth down to `tol` off the floor, and `lugs` lugs, each `lug_angle` of arc from 0 deg anticlockwise (then every 360 / `lugs`), whose upper flank is the socket's flank raised `preload`, rounded `radius` at the root and `radius` - `tol` at the tip. Each lug's leading end (at 0 deg: the plug turns clockwise to lock) is lowered `lead`, running out over `lead_angle` (a smooth loft through a few sections; its angles are fixed when the plug is built). The flange's underside has a dimple over each detent bump, `tol` over.
Twist sockettwist_socketThe female half of a twist lock: a round boss `body_d` across and `body_t` deep under its face (z = 0), centred on the z axis, with a revolved taper socket in it. A detent bump per lug stands `detent_h` proud of the face, for the plug flange's dimples.

DovetaildovetailThe dovetail section of the rails and their housings: a tongue `depth` deep whose flanks lean out at `angle` to its tip face, so it is wider at the tip than at the root and can't pull out of its slot. It is `width` across where calipers read it (between vertical tangents to the tip radii), and everything else is worked from that. The tip corners are rounded `tip_radius`, the root corners `root_radius`.
